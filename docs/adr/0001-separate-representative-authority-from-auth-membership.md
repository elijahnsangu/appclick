# Separate representative authority from authentication membership

Better Auth organization membership is a useful authentication and session projection, but it cannot prove that an individual has current, independently reviewed authority to act for a Business.
Representative Authorization and Representative Permissions are the marketplace source of truth, and organization membership may be created, changed, or removed only as a projection of those approved domain records.
This separation preserves Business-scoped evidence, lifecycle, Independent Review, recovery, and audit guarantees even if the authentication provider or membership model changes.
